# OpenIoTData visual direction

## THESIS

Open sensor data should feel surveyed, legible and ready to reuse. The interface refuses both the generic SaaS hero and the always-live telemetry dashboard; discovery itself is the first viewport.

## OWN-WORLD

A light topographic survey sheet: mineral off-white ground, cartographic navy ink, graphite secondary text, accessible mineral green actions, muted cyan geography and restrained vermilion warnings. Fine contour lines, coordinate ticks and registration marks carry structure without becoming decoration.

## STORY

A visitor immediately searches or filters, sees where data was captured, compares trustworthy metadata and opens a reproducible dataset. A publisher follows the same visual grammar through upload, inference, mapping and publication states.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

A slim global nav caps a split discovery workspace: task and filters at left; live interactive map at right; the first dataset rows remain visible below. Search is the dominant control and publication is the secondary global action.
